# Duet

The store-safe audiobook player with read-along: your audiobooks and ebooks on this phone and
your own Audiobookshelf, in one library. No sources, no debrid, no AI.

- Screens: `duet/` (built from Hearth); player, reader and connections are Audiohub's (`src/`).
- Build: a commit with `[build duet]` in its message. Releases: pre-releases tagged `duet-v…`.
- Icons / splash: `node duet/make-icons.mjs` → `duet/android-res/`.
- v1.0.1: step 1 — base, look, first open, Read-along tab (read-along view comes in step 2).

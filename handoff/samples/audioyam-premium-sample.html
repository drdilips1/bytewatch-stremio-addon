What changes, everywhere in the app

Type with character

Fraunces (already in the app) for the greeting, book titles and section heads; Figtree for everything else. A short label above each section ("Pick up where you left off") in small spaced capitals. Times and percentages in even-width numbers so they don't jiggle while playing.

Covers that look like books

A faint spine shadow and light edge on the left, a soft top shine, and a two-layer shadow (a crisp one where it touches, a long soft one below). Book-shaped (2:3) covers on shelves instead of squares where the cover is tall.

Sheen and depth

Cards get a 1-pixel top highlight and a gently fading fill instead of a flat grey box. The banner gets a slow diagonal light sweep. Main buttons are glossy pills with a soft glow in your accent colour.

Colour from the cover

The banner and book page pick up the cover's own colours as a soft glow behind it (the app already reads cover colours). The rest of the app keeps your palette.

Quiet texture

A barely-there film grain over the background so big dark areas feel like paper, not plastic. Off for AMOLED black and in Reduce motion / plain mode.

Room to breathe

One spacing rhythm: 20 px side margins, 30 px between sections, nothing touching the edges. Fewer icons in the top bar (Ask moves into the search page), and the long tagline goes, so the name is never squeezed.

Floating glass tab bar

The tab bar floats as a frosted glass pill above the bottom with a small glowing dot under the current tab; lists fade out under it instead of being cut off.

Little things

Pressed buttons sink slightly; rows fade in one after another; loading shapes match the real cards; empty shelves get a friendly line and one clear button instead of blank space; a big first letter on book descriptions.

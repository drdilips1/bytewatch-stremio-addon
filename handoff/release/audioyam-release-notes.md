# AudioYam — what's new

Everything since the last release (also in the current test build).

## 📚 Comics & manga
- **Comics in AudioYam:** add the *Audiohub Comics* addon (Settings → Addons) to search GetComics, Weeb Central, the Internet Archive and your library's Comics Plus. Every comic has its own page with cover, publisher, year, size and blurb.
- **A real comic reader** for CBZ, CBR, PDF and online chapters:
  - page by page or long scroll, right-to-left for manga, zoom;
  - themes (OLED black, paper, sepia), page-turn effects, two pages side by side on tablets, volume-key page turns.
- **Manga series read as one book,** chapter after chapter, with chapters you can download for offline.
- **Long-press a page:** translate it, explain it, or ask who's who with your AI key, spoiler-free. You can also save panels as memories.
- **A Comics shelf** in Library: Reading, Plan to read, On hold, Completed, Favourites.

## 🧲 Your own torrent app (no TorBox or Real-Debrid needed) — Android
- **Settings → Torrent app:** choose Flud, LibreTorrent or any torrent app on your phone, and the folder it downloads to.
- **Play** (or **Read** for ebooks) on AudiobookBay and other torrent results sends the book to that app with one tap.
- **AudioYam shows the download's progress bar and %** right on the result.
- **It plays by itself** (or opens the ebook in the reader) as soon as the download is complete.
- **No battery cost:** AudioYam only looks at the folder while it's open on screen, never in the background.
- If you delete a download, AudioYam notices and offers to get it again.

## 🎧 Listening
- **X-Ray who's who:** characters with their status at your point in the book, what changed since you last listened, a relationship map and pictures. Nothing from later in the book.
- **Talk:** ask the book a question out loud and hear a spoiler-free answer; playback then carries on.
- **Whisper Notes:** record your own voice at a moment in a book and hear it again when you get back there.
- **Time Machine:** search everything you've heard and kept, across all your books.
- **Come back to…** on Home: a book you left a few weeks ago, with the story so far.
- **Listener Challenges** each month in Stats.

## 🔎 Finding books
- **Discover → Free:** free public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox and Project Gutenberg, by language and genre.
- **A book no longer in your phone folder, TorBox or Real-Debrid** shows where to get it again, not an error.

## 💾 Storage
- **Comics and ebooks are kept once, in your phone's Downloads → Audiohub → Comics / Ebooks** (Android):
  - they show in the Files app, other apps can open them, and they stay after reinstalling;
  - there's no second copy inside the app;
  - the ones kept inside before move there by themselves.
- **Settings → Storage** shows what AudioYam keeps on your phone, biggest first, with Remove. Old update files are now cleaned up automatically.

## ✨ Look
- **A more polished finish:** serif titles, section labels, covers with a spine, a soft shine on cards and buttons, a floating tab bar, a fine paper grain. Your colours stay. Plain look: Settings → Appearance → Finish.

## 📱 iPhone & iPad
- **Offline downloads** (in the Files app), **Send to Kindle** straight from the app, and **every iPhone voice** for reading ebooks aloud, with read-aloud continuing on the lock screen.
- **Save puts ebooks and comics in the Files app;** adding your own audiobooks works with .mp4 and iCloud files.

## 🛠 Better
- **Your whole Audiobookshelf library** with its real count (it stopped at 500).
- **The right author** on author pages.
- **Deleted bookmarks and highlights** stay deleted on all devices.
- **Music in your cloud** stays out of the Home banner.
- **"AudiobookBay / LibGen is down"** no longer shows when they simply have no results for a book.

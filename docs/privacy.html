Narrato privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Narrato reads books, documents, web pages and text aloud on your phone. It has no accounts and no sign-in. This page explains what data the app handles.

Your documents stay on your phone

Files, text and photos you add are stored only inside the app on your device. Reading aloud, text recognition in photos and the natural voices all run on your phone. The app does not upload your documents anywhere.

What the app downloads

  • Web pages you add by link are fetched directly from that website.
  • Voice packs you choose to download come from GitHub (github.com). GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first; you can change your choice any time under Menu → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses data from apps that show its ads.

Problem reports

The app keeps a small error log on your phone. It is sent to no one unless you choose "Report a problem" and share it yourself (for example by email). The report contains the app version, phone model, settings and the log, but no document text.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a document by long-pressing it in the library, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.
